The app

Tchak Darts keeps score for your darts games (Cricket, cut-throat, 301, 501), tracks statistics and can show the game on a screen. It is provided as is, without warranty: double-check scores that matter (tournaments, bets) yourself.

Free, ads and premium

The app is free and funded by advertising (a banner and optional videos). The premium purchase, one-time and non-consumable, removes the banner and unlocks games, voices, themes and advanced stats for the store account used to buy it. It can be restored from the app’s shop. Refunds follow App Store or Google Play rules.

TV screen mode

The TV page and the "Follow the game" QR code let anyone who sees them follow the game read-only. Only turn TV mode on if you are fine with first names and scores being visible to the people around.

Your data

It stays on your phone (see the privacy policy). Export a backup before moving to a new phone.
